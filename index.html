Gallopwood

Ride, fly and explore a cosy horse world made for kids.

Plays 100% offline No ads No chat or strangers No in-app purchases Collects no data

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

What kids can do

Make a rider, choose a horse and style its coat, mane and saddle. Earn magical wings and fly above the clouds. Build a cosy home, explore endless villages, swim, sail and play mini-games with friendly villagers, all without the internet.

Why parents like it

Gallopwood never connects to the internet. There are no ads, no accounts, no chat and no typing, and it asks for no permissions. Grown-up features like save backups sit behind a parental gate. Want to try first? Gallopwood Lite is free and lets your child carry their progress into the full game.
