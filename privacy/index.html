Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026. Applies to Gallopwood and Gallopwood Lite on iOS, iPadOS, Android and macOS.

Gallopwood is a game for children that plays entirely offline. We built it so it collects nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Gallopwood does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information or usage data. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no advertising and no analytics, and it never connects to the internet.

What stays on the device

The game saves your child's progress (their rider, horse, home and items) in the app's own storage on the device. This data never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Backups

A grown-up can choose to export a backup file through a parental gate. The file is saved wherever the grown-up chooses, such as the Files app or a cloud drive they pick. We never see or receive it.

The store link in Gallopwood Lite

Gallopwood Lite has one button that opens the full game's App Store or Google Play page. It sits behind a parental gate and sends no information.

Permissions

Gallopwood asks for no permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no information from anyone, Gallopwood complies with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR's rules on children's data.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
