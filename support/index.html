Common questions

Does Gallopwood need the internet?

No. It plays fully offline, so it's great for car trips and flights.

How do I open the grown-ups menu?

Open the pause menu and tap Grown-ups. Hold the button for 3 seconds. Then read the sum written in words, such as "twenty-four plus thirteen", type the answer on the number pad and tap OK. The keys move around each time, so children can't learn the pattern.

I typed the wrong answer. What now?

The grown-ups menu takes a short break after a wrong answer. Wait about 30 seconds, then try again with a new sum. The break ends when the time is up or when the app is closed and reopened.

How do I back up my child's progress?

In the Grown-ups menu, choose Save backup and keep the file somewhere safe, such as the Files app or your cloud drive. To bring it back, choose Restore backup and pick the file.

Can we move from Gallopwood Lite to the full game?

Yes. In Lite, choose Save backup in the Grown-ups menu. In the full game, choose Restore backup and pick that file, and everything carries over. On iPhone or iPad, first use the Files app to copy the backup from the Gallopwood Lite folder into the Gallopwood folder.

The game feels slow on an older tablet.

Gallopwood picks lighter graphics automatically on older devices. Closing other apps helps. If it still stutters, email us your device model.

Are there ads or purchases?

No. Gallopwood has no ads and no in-app purchases. Gallopwood Lite is free; the full game is a one-time purchase.
