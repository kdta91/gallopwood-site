Support

Need help with Gallopwood or Gallopwood Lite? Email kampong.dev@gmail.com and we'll get back to you. Please tell us your device and what happened.

Common questions

Does Gallopwood need the internet?

No. It plays fully offline, so it's great for car trips and flights.

How do I open the grown-ups menu?

Open the pause menu and tap Grown-ups. Hold the button for 3 seconds, then answer the word sum. Children can't get past it by accident.

How do I back up my child's progress?

In the Grown-ups menu, choose Export backup and save the file somewhere safe, such as the Files app or your cloud drive. To restore, choose Import backup and pick the file.

Can we move from Gallopwood Lite to the full game?

Yes. In Lite, export a backup from the Grown-ups menu. In the full game, import it, and everything carries over.

The game feels slow on an older tablet.

Gallopwood picks lighter graphics automatically on older devices. Closing other apps helps. If it still stutters, email us your device model.

Are there ads or purchases?

No. Gallopwood has no ads and no in-app purchases. Gallopwood Lite is free; the full game is a one-time purchase.
